About Dhage Travels

Maharashtra's most trusted premium bus service — connecting hearts since 1985.

40+
YEARS

Dhage Travels has been connecting people across Maharashtra for over four decades. What started as a single bus route from Majalgaon to Mumbai in 1985 has grown into a premier network of luxury bus services touching Pune, Beed, Parbhani, Nanded, Georai, Sonpeth and beyond. We don't just carry passengers — we carry stories, dreams, and families home.

Our Values

The principles that define every journey we operate.

Safety First

Every coach undergoes daily safety inspections. All drivers are certified and trained for defensive driving, ensuring you always arrive safely.

Premium Comfort

From air-conditioned Volvo sleepers to recliner seats — we believe every passenger deserves a comfortable journey, regardless of distance.

Punctuality

We respect your time. Our operations team monitors every trip in real-time to ensure buses depart and arrive on schedule, every day.

Community Roots

Born in Majalgaon, built by the community. We take pride in serving the towns and villages of Maharashtra, connecting them to opportunities.

Our Journey

Milestones that shaped who we are today.

1985

The First Bus

Mr. Dhage launched the very first route — Majalgaon to Mumbai — with a single bus and a vision to connect rural Maharashtra to the city.

1998

Expanding Routes

Routes to Parbhani, Nanded, and Beed were added. The fleet grew to 12 buses serving 5 cities across Marathwada.

2008

Going Luxury

Introduced our iconic AC Sleeper coaches — the first luxury overnight bus in the Marathwada region — setting a new benchmark for comfort.

2026

Going Digital

Launched our new digital booking platform, allowing passengers to search, choose seats, and pay online — making travel easier than ever.
